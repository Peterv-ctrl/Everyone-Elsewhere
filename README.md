# Everyone, Elsewhere
[Play the game](https://peterv-ctrl.github.io/Everyone-Elsewhere/)
Everyone who heard the broadcast is gone. Almost everyone.
Everyone, Elsewhere is a free third-person space exploration game for your browser. Fly the Warp-c8 across worlds with your dog Hailey, follow a trail of ancient monoliths, brave storms, explore caves, and find out where everyone went.
The game features worlds across three star systems, changing weather, caves, hidden weapons, and an original soundtrack.
Open the play link in a browser with an internet connection.
